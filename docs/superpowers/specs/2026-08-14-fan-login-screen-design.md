## Experience contract

- Center the text-only `FANFOLIO` wordmark, headline, and two-line supporting copy.
- Use the four-member Dreamscape image as the visual focus with a soft lavender halo and a bottom fade into the page.
- Show social providers in this order: Apple, Google, Kakao, Naver.
- Keep Google and Kakao OAuth behavior intact. Apple and Naver remain visible but communicate that setup is pending when selected.
- Keep email magic-link login intact, initially collapsed behind the single `이메일로 로그인` CTA so the first screen matches the reference.
- Preserve keyboard focus, button labels, browser autofill metadata, error/status announcements, and small-screen scrolling.

## Visual tokens

- Canvas: warm off-white with a subtle lavender radial glow.
- Ink: deep navy; secondary copy: cool gray; accent: violet-to-blue.
- Buttons: white, 16px radius, thin lavender-gray border, soft elevation.
- Main content width: 430px maximum; no device frame or bottom navigation.
